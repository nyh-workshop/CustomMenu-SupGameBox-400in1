english.png here replaces the background of the game list (256 x 240, NES colours).
Start from original/english.png. The list text is drawn over columns 8-28, rows 5-23 in colour index 3 (white).
Rules: rombuilder/README.md, "Wallpaper" (checked by wallpaper/wallpaper.py); original/english_guide.png shows in red where the list is drawn. Without english.png the base image's background is used. In the stripped base its space picture (Street Fighter 2010
tiles) is removed and left black: original/english.png is that background as it is now. Make your own.

colors.txt sets the list colours (the names, the colour around them, the cursor, the title of the original background);
nes_colours.png shows the 64 NES colours with their numbers. Around the names the screen shows colour 0 of the picture:
by default the colour that covers most of your picture under the list lines.
